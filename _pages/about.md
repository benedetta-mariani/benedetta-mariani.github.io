<p>I’m a postdoctoral researcher at the <a href="https://www.liphlab.com/">Laboratory of Interdisciplinary Physics</a> and the <a href="https://pnc.unipd.it/">Padova Neuroscience Center</a>, with a PhD in Neuroscience and a background in the physics of complex systems. I combine mechanistic modelling and data analysis across scales to uncover the principles underlying neural activity and develop experimentally testable predictions. My research explores how neural dynamics support computation, with a particular interest in criticality, oscillations and synchronization.</p>
